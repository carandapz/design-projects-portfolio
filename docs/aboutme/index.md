#About Me

Hello all, my name is Carlos Aranda Perez. 
As an engineer, I aspire to see change happen, change that will bring improvements and facilitate the lives of others, perhaps even bring joy. The joy I seek comes in the form of machinery, gears turning, systems built decades ago lasting the test of time, yet somehow there are also miniscule designs that often go unnoticed yet bring unprecedented amounts of ease during day to day life. To me, that was the beckoning I perceived when choosing to become a Mechanical Engineer, fascinated by the innovations made through just trying to make life easier. The type of engineer I am becoming is one who values problem solving, and the ability to turn ideas into practical solutions. Mechanical engineering interests me because it combines design, and creativity to solve problems that affect the world around us. I am especially interested in understanding how things work and designing solutions that are both functional and useful. Through my education, I am learning that engineering is not simply about finding an answer, but more so understanding the problem, considering different possibilities, and making decisions based on evidence and sound reasoning. I want to become an engineer who can approach challenges with curiosity and persistence while also considering the people who will ultimately use the solutions I help create. As I continue developing my technical skills, I also want to strengthen my ability to communicate ideas, collaborate with others, and explain the reasoning behind my designs, since even the most well thought out solution loses its credibility if it cannot be clearly understood by others, especially those who do not specialize in certain areas of study or work. This course will help foster these skills and in turn guide me to my goal. My goal is to become a well rounded mechanical engineer who can contribute meaningful solutions to real world problems and continue learning throughout my career.


#*What does it mean to defend an engineering decision and do you currently know how to do it?*

To defend an engineering decision means being able to clearly explain why a particular solution, design choice, or approach was selected and support that decision with evidence, calculations, data, requirements, and engineering principles. I believe defending a decision is more than simply saying that I think something is the best option. I need to be able to explain my reasoning and respond to questions or alternative viewpoints. At this point, I am still developing this ability. I understand the importance of supporting my decisions with technical reasoning, but I do not believe I have completely grasped how to defend an engineering decision confidently and professionally. I expect that through my coursework and experiences, I will become better at communicating my reasoning, evaluating alternatives, and using evidence to justify my decisions. I hope that when I revisit this statement at the end of the semester, I will be able to recognize meaningful growth in this area.
