#About Me

Hello all, my name is Carlos Aranda Perez. 
As an engineer, I aspire to see change happen, change that will bring improvements and facilitate the lives of others, perhaps even bring joy. The joy I seek comes in the form of machinery, gears turning, systems built decades ago lasting the test of time, yet somehow there are also miniscule designs that often go unnoticed yet bring unprecedented amounts of ease during day to day life. To me, that was the beckoning I perceived when choosing to become a Mechanical Engineer, fascinated by the innovations made through just trying to make life easier. The type of engineer I am becoming is one who values problem-solving, and the ability to turn ideas into practical solutions. Mechanical engineering interests me because it combines design, and creativity to solve problems that affect the world around us. I am especially interested in understanding how things work and designing solutions that are both functional and useful. Through my education, I am learning that engineering is not simply about finding an answer, but more so understanding the problem, considering different possibilities, and making decisions based on evidence and sound reasoning. I want to become an engineer who can approach challenges with curiosity and persistence while also considering the people who will ultimately use the solutions I help create. As I continue developing my technical skills, I also want to strengthen my ability to communicate ideas, collaborate with others, and explain the reasoning behind my designs, since even the most well thought out solution loses its credibility if it cannot be clearly understood by others, especially those who do not specialize in certain areas of study or work. This course will help foster these skills and in turn guide me
